About

I ask why until the answer is useful.

A passionate, detail-oriented designer with a proven track record of delivering user-centric solutions at scale. Someone who will keep asking about the edge case you were hoping nobody would raise.

Every portfolio has the first sentence. Only one of them is true.

I design products — interfaces, flows, and the systems that hold them together. Mostly for the web, mostly for tools people have to use rather than want to use, which is where design either earns its keep or doesn’t.

I started as the only designer on a product, which taught me the thing no course does: you have to defend a decision to someone who disagrees with it, weekly, in a call. Since then I’ve worked in open source, where the disagreement is public and permanent, and on client work where the brief changes on Thursday.

I write fiction too — novels and serialised stories, published on a schedule. People treat that as the unrelated hobby. It isn’t. Both jobs are about deciding what to cut, holding a reader’s attention through a sequence, and knowing that the sentence in the empty state is doing more work than the illustration above it.

The short version: I am not precious about my work, I am extremely precious about the user’s time.
